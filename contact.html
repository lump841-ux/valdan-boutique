Frequently Asked Questions ♡

Are your fabrics hypoallergenic and GOTS-certified?

▾

Yes, absolutely. Every Val & Dan garment is crafted from GOTS-certified organic cotton. Our fabrics are independently lab-tested to be free from harsh chemicals, synthetic dyes, and common allergens — making them safe for even the most sensitive little skin.

How does sizing work for babies and toddlers?

▾

Our collections are sized by age range (Newborn, 0-3M, 3-6M, 6-12M, 12-18M, 18-24M, 2-3Y) and each product page includes a detailed fit guide. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for room to grow.

What is your returns and exchange policy?

▾

We want every Val & Dan purchase to feel right. If something doesn't work, unworn items in original packaging can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery. Exchanges are always free.
