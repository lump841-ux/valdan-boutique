Our Humble Beginnings

Honoring the softness of delicate skin

Val & Dan began at a kitchen table, not a boardroom. Their grandmother had spent decades sewing for her own children and grandchildren, keeping every scrap of soft cotton "just in case." It was watching her two granddaughters, Valerie and Danny, grow up right in front of her — one a careful little helper with a needle, the other always redesigning her own hand-me-downs — that gave her the idea to turn a lifetime of sewing into something real. She started making tiny rompers and blankets on weekends, inspired by the two of them, and what began as gifts for family and friends slowly became too many orders for a kitchen table to hold.

The name Val & Dan is simply theirs — a grandmother's tribute to the two granddaughters who inspired her to start it all. Every collection still carries a little of both girls in spirit: the softness a newborn deserves, and the playful, present-day eye for color and style only a granddaughter could bring. We don't chase trends — we make the same kind of timeless, gentle pieces Grandma always made, just now with their names on the door.
