Our Humble Beginnings

Honoring the softness of delicate skin

At Val & Dan, we believe every stitch tells a story. Born from a mother's desire to dress her children in fabrics as gentle as a woodland breeze, our collections are crafted from 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton, plant-dyed linens, and responsibly sourced wool. Every garment is designed in our Portland studio and made by artisan partners who share our commitment to fair wages and sustainable practices. We don't chase trends — we create timeless pieces that grow with your little ones, meant to be worn, loved, and passed down.

Every hand-embroidered rose, delicate ribbon, and organic seam is designed to accompany your children through all their lovely milestones. We believe baby clothing shouldn't just be pretty—it should support their natural impulse to explore, stretch, and dream.
