Our Humble Beginnings

Honoring the softness of delicate skin

Val & Dan began with a grandmother who simply wanted the best for her two granddaughters. Shopping for Valerie and Danny, she kept running into the same problem — clothing that looked lovely on a hanger but felt scratchy, cheaply made, or wasn't as soft as a little one's skin deserves. So she started searching further: seeking out organic fabrics, better stitching, and softer weaves, holding every piece to the standard she wanted for her own girls. What began as sourcing for family soon became a mission worth sharing.

The name Val & Dan is simply theirs — a grandmother's tribute to the two granddaughters who inspired her to start it all. Every piece we carry is chosen with the same care she once put into picking out something for Valerie and Danny: the softness a newborn deserves, and the playful, present-day eye for color and style only a granddaughter could bring. We don't chase trends — we curate the same kind of timeless, gentle quality Grandma always insisted on, just now with their names on the door.
